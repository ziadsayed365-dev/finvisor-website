COD e-commerce · 180 days

EGP 92,309 lost on shipping in 180 days

Across 4,105 orders the store charged EGP 75–120 for delivery depending on destination — and the courier billed more than that in all 26 governorates it shipped to. The gap ran at EGP 27.83 per delivered order.

  • 4,105 orders reviewed

Pilgrimage & specialty goods

Shipping repriced governorate by governorate

  • 91.7% delivery rate
  • EGP 45,903 recovered
  • 72% of the gap closed
