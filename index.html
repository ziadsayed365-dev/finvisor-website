Problem 1 of 5

Know Your Real Profit

The problem: your store shows sales, not profit. Product cost, courier fees, failed deliveries, returns and ad spend all come out after the sale, so the number you see is not the number you keep.

How we fix it

  • Daily P&L & Management Reporting

    A full income statement rebuilt every day: revenue, product cost, courier fees, ad spend, contribution margin and net profit, by day, month and product.

  • Daily & Weekly Performance Reports

    Short daily and weekly reports with review meetings, so you catch a drop in margin or delivery rate the day it starts.

  • Expense Control & Cost Optimisation

    We record your expenses and cash collections, analyse where the money goes, and show you which costs to cut or restructure.

  • Budgeting & Variance Analysis

    Monthly budgets built on your own margins and delivery rates, compared with actual results so gaps are caught early.

Problem 2 of 5

Stop the Products That Lose Money

The problem: a product can sell well and still lose money on every order once its courier fee, packaging, returns and ad cost are added. Without numbers per product, you keep scaling the wrong ones.

How we fix it

  • Product Costing (Bill of Materials)

    Every product costed from its raw materials, so when one material's price changes, every product that uses it is repriced.

  • SKU Profitability Analysis

    Net profit per product after courier, packaging, returns and ads, so you know which products to scale, reprice or stop.

  • Pricing Strategy & Sales Targets

    Prices set from your real costs to hit your target margin, and the sales you need each month to reach your profit goal.

  • Shipping & Courier Cost Analysis

    Courier fees, return penalties and delivery rates by governorate, compared with what your customers actually pay for shipping.

Problem 3 of 5

Move Your Ad Budget to the Winners

The problem: ad platforms report ROAS on orders placed, not on profit. Budget gets split across products by feel, so money keeps flowing to ads that look good but lose you money.

How we fix it

  • Real ROAS per Product

    Ad performance tracked product by product, measured against delivered orders and real profit, not the platform's view.

  • Break-Even ROAS & CPA Limits

    The lowest ROAS and the highest CPA each product can reach before its ads stop making money.

  • Ad Budget Reallocation

    We show you where to move spend: away from products that burn cash, and towards the products with the highest profitable ROAS.

  • Marketing Agency Alignment

    We work with your agency or in-house team to set ROAS targets and ad budgets tied to real profit.

Problem 4 of 5

Buy the Right Stock, in the Right Amount

The problem: buy too much and your cash sits on the shelf as dead stock. Buy too little and your best products sell out while your ads keep running. Most purchase orders are based on guesswork.

How we fix it

  • Purchase Quantities per Product

    How much to buy of each product, based on its profitability and how fast it sells, so your cash goes into stock that moves.

  • Stock Turnover & Days of Cover

    How many days of stock you hold per product and when to reorder, so you avoid both overstock and stock-outs.

  • Raw Material Purchasing System

    For brands that manufacture: a system that calculates the raw materials to buy to cover your planned finished goods.

Problem 5 of 5

See Where Your Profit Actually Is

The problem: your P&L says you made a profit, but the cash isn't in the bank. It may be sitting as COD with the courier, as stock on the shelf, or already gone on paying debts. Without knowing where it is, you can't tell how much you can safely take out.

How we fix it

  • Equity Calculation

    What your business is worth on any day: cash and bank balances, courier wallet and payment gateways, COD still with the courier, and stock at cost, minus everything you owe.

  • Profit Reconciliation

    The change in your equity is checked against the profit your P&L reports, so you know the profit is real and exactly where it sits.

  • Cash Flow Forecast & Safe Withdrawals

    When money comes in and goes out, your burn rate, and how much profit you can withdraw without hurting operations.

  • What-If Scenario Planning

    See the impact of a decision before you make it: a new product, a different courier, a loan or a price change.
