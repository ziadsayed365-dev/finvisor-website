Pricing Approaches

Three Ways to Set a Price

Share of companies using each approach, averaged across two decades of pricing surveys in the US, Europe and Asia (Hinterhuber, Journal of Business Strategy, 2008).

Used in this tool
37%of companies price this way

Cost-based pricing

Start from what each delivered order really costs you, then add the margin you want on top.

  • Product, packaging, ads and fixed expenses
  • Failed deliveries covered by the orders that arrive
  • The net margin you choose
44%of companies price this way

Competitor-based pricing

Check what similar stores charge and price close to them. Quick to set, but their costs, delivery rate and ad spend aren't yours, so the same price can make them money and lose you money.

17%of companies price this way

Value-based pricing

Price by what customers are willing to pay for the product. It can earn higher margins, but without knowing your full costs you can't tell whether a price actually makes money.
