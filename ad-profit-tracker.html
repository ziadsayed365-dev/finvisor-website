Why our numbers are honest

Why we show less than Meta, on purpose

Meta counts sales it can't really prove. We only credit an order when there's a real ad click behind it, and then we subtract the true costs. Fewer sales claimed, but every number you can stand on.

  • Meta counts view-through sales, people who only saw an ad and never clicked.
  • It adds modelled and cross-device purchases it estimates rather than records.
  • It often claims repeat and organic customers who would have bought anyway.
  • We credit the last ad click within 7 days, so each order belongs to exactly one campaign, totals always add up.

We also catch sales you'd otherwise lose

When you connect, we check your ads for missing tracking tags, the number-one reason real sales go uncounted. You get the exact template to paste, so every order that came from an ad is captured.
